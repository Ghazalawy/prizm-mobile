First-time install on Android

  1. Tap Download APK above (or scan the QR with your phone camera).
  2. When the download finishes, tap the prizm-mobile.apk notification.
  3. Android may ask: "Allow this source to install apps?" — tap Settings, enable Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. Open the app once installed.
  5. Sign in with your Prizm CRM credentials.

Updating to a newer build

  • Open this page on the phone and tap Download APK again.
  • Tap the new APK. Android will offer to update the existing install — your login stays.

Troubleshooting

  • "App not installed" — uninstall any previous version first, then retry.
  • Can't reach server — confirm the phone has internet and that ms.prizm-energy.com is reachable from a browser.
  • Login fails — verify your CRM account still works at ms.prizm-energy.com.
